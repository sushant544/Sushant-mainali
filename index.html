About LeBron

LeBron James is one of the greatest basketball players of all time. Born December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio, he has won multiple NBA championships, earned MVP honors, and led his teams to numerous Finals appearances. His versatility, leadership, and work ethic have defined a career that spans decades.
